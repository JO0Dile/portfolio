About

Bio

I'm an AI & Robotics Engineering student who enjoys the uncomfortable middle ground between research and software engineering.

My work usually starts with a question — can a model learn this faster? Can this workflow be made offline-first? Can a small local LLM replace a cloud call? — and ends as a shipped tool that someone actually uses.

I care about clean systems, honest interfaces, and code that survives being read by the next person. When I'm not building, I'm reading papers, breaking my own training runs, or refactoring something that was already working.
